
**“Gang of Four” (GoF)**, the authors of the classic book _“Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software”_.

In software engineering, “GoF” usually means:

- Erich Gamma
- Richard Helm
- Ralph Johnson
- John Vlissides

Their book catalogues 23 fundamental object-oriented design patterns (like Singleton, Observer, Factory Method, Strategy, etc.) and is a cornerstone reference in OOP and design patterns.
