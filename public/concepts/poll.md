A poll is the simplest instrument of audience agency online: a question broadcast to everyone, answered by clicks, aggregated into a result that decides what happens next. Inherited from television votes and streaming chats, the poll turns listeners into a distributed, quantified chorus. In internet concerts it can select the next section, tune a parameter, or split the audience into factions — raising the classic questions of participation: who frames the options, how much does a vote really decide, and does the majority always make the better music?
