A glitch is a short-lived fault in a system — a corrupted frame, a stuttering buffer, a misread file — that briefly exposes the machinery beneath the interface. In music and visual art, the glitch became a genre: artists provoke, capture, and compose with errors, valuing the moment when a medium fails and shows its material truth. On the networked stage, glitches are unavoidable guests; the aesthetic question is whether they are suppressed, tolerated, or invited as performers in their own right.
